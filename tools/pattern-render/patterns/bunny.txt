HEAD (white)
Rnd 1: 6 sc in magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc in each st around (12)
Rnd 3: *sc, inc* repeat 6 times (18)
Rnd 4: *2 sc, inc* repeat 6 times (24)
Rnd 5: *3 sc, inc* repeat 6 times (30)
Rnds 6-11: sc around (30)
Place safety eyes between rounds 9 and 10, 7 sts apart.
Rnd 12: *3 sc, dec* repeat 6 times (24)
Rnd 13: *2 sc, dec* repeat 6 times (18)
Stuff the head.
Rnd 14: *sc, dec* repeat 6 times (12)
Rnd 15: dec around (6)
Fasten off.
BODY (white)
Rnd 1: 6 sc in magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc around (12)
Rnd 3: *sc, inc* x6 (18)
Rnd 4: *2 sc, inc* x6 (24)
Rnds 5-9: sc around (24)
Rnd 10: *2 sc, dec* x6 (18)
Rnds 11-12: sc around (18)
Fasten off, leave a long tail. Stuff.
EARS (make 2) (pink)
Rnd 1: 6 sc in magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: *sc, inc* x3 (9)
Rnd 3: *2 sc, inc* x3 (12)
Rnds 4-12: sc around (12)
Rnd 13: *2 sc, dec* x3 (9)
Fasten off. Do not stuff.
ARMS (make 2) (white)
Rnd 1: 6 sc in magic ring (6)
Rnds 2-7: sc around (6)
Fasten off.
LEGS (make 2) (white)
Rnd 1: 6 sc in magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc around (12)
Rnds 3-5: sc around (12)
Rnd 6: dec around (6)
ASSEMBLY
Sew the ears to the top of the head between rounds 3 and 4.
Sew the head to the body.
Sew the arms to the sides of the body between rounds 10 and 11.
Sew the legs to the bottom of the body.
