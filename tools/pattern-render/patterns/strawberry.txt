Berry (red)
Round 1: 4 sc in magic ring = 4
Round 2: (sc, inc) twice = 6
Round 3: (sc, inc) 3 times = 9
Round 4: (2 sc, inc) 3 times = 12
Round 5: (sc, inc) 6 times = 18
Round 6: (2 sc, inc) 6 times = 24
Round 7: (3 sc, inc) 6 times = 30
Rounds 8-12: sc around = 30
Round 13: (3 sc, dec) 6 times = 24
Round 14: (2 sc, dec) 6 times = 18
Round 15: (sc, dec) 6 times = 12
Round 16: dec 6 times = 6
Leaves (green)
Round 1: 6 sc in magic ring = 6
Round 2: inc in every stitch = 12
Round 3: (sc, inc) 6 times = 18
Sew the leaves on top of the berry.
