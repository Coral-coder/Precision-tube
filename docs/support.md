# StitchStep Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email **[your support email]**. We usually reply within a couple of days.

**Common questions**

- **How do I open a YouTube video?** In the YouTube app tap Share → StitchStep, or Share →
  Copy Link and tap Paste in StitchStep's Videos tab.
- **A video won't play.** Some uploaders don't allow their videos to play outside YouTube.
  Use "Open in YouTube" for those.
- **Frame steps look uneven.** Set the video's frame rate under ⋯ → Frame rate (most videos
  are 30 or 24 fps).
- **Where are my patterns and counters stored?** Only on your device. Deleting the app
  deletes them.
- **I bought Pro on another device.** Tools → Restore Purchases.
