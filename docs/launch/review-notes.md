# Notes for App Review

Paste into App Store Connect → the version → App Review Information → Notes. This assumes the
store-safe version from README section 1.

> StitchStep helps people learn crochet and knitting from videos by stepping through them frame by frame, looping a section, and keeping a row count alongside.
>
> YouTube videos: StitchStep does not download, save or convert any YouTube content. YouTube videos play in YouTube's official embedded player (the IFrame Player API) exactly as YouTube presents it, with its branding and controls intact. Our app only uses the API's documented functions (play, pause, seek, playback rate, captions). Users open a video by sharing it from the YouTube app or pasting a link.
>
> Own videos: users can open videos from Photos or Files; these play locally with AVPlayer.
>
> In-app purchase: StitchStep Pro unlocks our own features (patterns, counters, calculators, precision tools for the user's own videos, Shape Studio). Watching YouTube videos is free and is not part of the purchase.
>
> No account or login is needed. To test: open the Videos tab, paste https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<a public crochet tutorial ID>, then use the frame buttons under the player. The Patterns and Tools tabs work offline.
