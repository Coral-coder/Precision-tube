**Description**:

> Ever watched a crochet video ten times and still missed where the hook went? StitchStep slows it right down.
>
> SEE EVERY STITCH
> • Step forward and back one frame at a time. Hold the button to keep stepping.
> • Slow motion, from quarter speed up to 2×.
> • A zoomable timeline: pinch until every frame has its own tick, then drag to scrub.
> • Repeat a section: mark where a stitch starts and ends and it loops while you copy it.
> • Bookmark the start of each row and jump straight back.
> • Captions, playlists that play on, and every video reopens where you stopped.
> • Landscape mode with see-through controls, and frame buttons on the screen edges.
>
> YOUR OWN VIDEOS, UP CLOSE
> • Open videos from Photos or Files and pinch to zoom right into the hands.
>
> FOLLOW YOUR PATTERN
> • Paste any pattern and tick off each row as you go, with a row counter built in.
> • Starter patterns: granny square, amigurumi ball, dishcloths, ribbed beanie, scarf.
> • A stitch dictionary with US and UK names.
>
> HANDY TOOLS
> • Row and stitch counters
> • Increase/decrease evenly calculator
> • Gauge calculator
> • Hook and needle sizes, yarn weights, US ↔ UK terms
> • Pick your own app color
>
> SHAPE STUDIO (EXPERIMENTAL)
> • Turn a shape or a 3D model into an amigurumi pattern, or see a round-by-round pattern in 3D before you start.
>
> StitchStep doesn't collect any data. Your patterns, counters and videos stay on your device.
