# StitchStep Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

StitchStep doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.

**What stays on your device.** Your saved videos list, playback positions, bookmarks,
patterns, row counters and settings are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. They are not sent
to us or anyone else, and they are removed if you delete the app.

**No accounts, analytics or ads.** StitchStep has no sign-in, no analytics or tracking tools,
and no advertising.

**YouTube.** When you play a YouTube video, it plays in YouTube's embedded player, which is
provided by YouTube and connects to YouTube's servers. The app also asks YouTube for a
video's title and thumbnail. YouTube's handling of that is covered by the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the
[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).

**Your own videos and 3D files.** Videos and 3D models you open are read on your device only
and never uploaded.

**Children.** StitchStep is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information
from anyone.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
